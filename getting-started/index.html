    Getting started and accessing the lab

    1. Open a browser on your provided laptop and go to https://dcloud.cisco.com

    2. Click Login at the top right corner and log in with your Cisco.com credentials.

    3. Once logged in, open a new browser tab and paste the event URL for your lab. The event URL is https://dcloud2-rtp.cisco.com/event/407958/access

    4. You will be automatically assigned to a lab pod, and you will be taken to the Lab topology page as shown below.

    5. Click on the User Workstation 1 icon on the topology page and it will bring up a fly-out window on left with Workstation 1 related information like IP Address, username, and password as shown below.

    6. Similarly, you can click on any other Virtual Machine on the topology page to get its respective details.

    7. In this lab, you will access Workstation(s) (through WebRDP) and you will be able to access the Webex app to complete the lab tasks.

    8. You can connect via a WebRDP connection from your classroom laptop.

    WebRDP

    To access Workstation(s) over WebRDP, click on the Workstation 1 icon on the topology page and when it brings up a fly-out window with workstation details. On the fly-out window go to click on the Remote Access > Web RDP. It will open a new browser tab and connect you to Workstation 1.

    Record your session details

    1. Once you are connected to Workstation 1, go to your dCloud lab topology page, click on the Info tab, and a fly-out window will open on the left side. Here you will find all your lab-related information like the Session ID, DNS Domain name. You will need all this information throughout this lab.

    Similarly, you can access Workstation 2 and Workstation 3.

    1. It would be a good idea to create a notepad (or notepad++) on your PC and document your session-related information like the Session ID, Domain name (DNS), Control Hub password, etc. This information will be required throughout the lab modules.

    Session ID: you will find it under the details tab, as shown above (Example 1168643)

    Control Hub password: dCloud8643! (dCloud + last four digits of session ID + !)

    Domain Name: Go to the details tab of your session and scroll down to see the DNS name. It will be in the cbXXX.dc-YY.com format.

    Now you can proceed with the lab modules.
